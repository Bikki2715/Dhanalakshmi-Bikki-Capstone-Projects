# Individual Reflection – DA-04 Financial Analytics and Risk Reporting System

**Name:** Dhanalakshmi Bikki  
**Project:** Financial Analytics and Risk Reporting System

## 1. Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I gained practical experience in financial data analysis, SQL querying, Python-based credit risk modeling, and dashboard development. I learned how to analyze financial statements, evaluate budget variances, and communicate business insights through interactive dashboards.

## 2. Tools and Technologies

I worked with Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, and Qlik Sense. I implemented Logistic Regression and Weight of Evidence (WoE) techniques to develop a credit risk scorecard and evaluate model performance.

## 3. Challenges Faced

One of my main challenges was working with large datasets and ensuring accurate financial calculations. I also faced limitations accessing Microsoft Excel Power Pivot and Power Automate. I used Power BI as an alternative for data modeling and dashboard development and implemented automated monthly PDF generation using Python and Windows Task Scheduler.

## 4. Individual Contribution

I independently worked on data preparation, financial analysis, SQL queries, credit risk modeling, dashboard development, report generation, testing, and project documentation. I also evaluated model performance and documented its limitations.

## 5. Conclusion

This project improved my technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills. It helped me understand how financial analytics and credit risk modeling can support business decision-making. I gained confidence in applying data analytics concepts to a practical business problem.
