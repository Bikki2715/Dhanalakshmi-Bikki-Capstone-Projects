# Dashboard User Guide
## Financial Analytics and Risk Reporting System

### 1. Power BI Dashboard

File: `Financial_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix`

**Financial Overview**
- View total revenue, expenses, net profit and profit margin.
- Analyze monthly net profit.
- Compare monthly revenue and expenses.

**Budget vs Actual**
- Compare budgeted and actual revenue.
- Compare budgeted and actual expenses.
- Review profit variance and variance percentage.

Use the available slicers to filter the dashboard.

### 2. Qlik Sense Dashboard

App: `DA-04 Financial Analytics`

The app contains three sheets:

1. **Financial Overview:** Financial KPIs and monthly performance.
2. **Budget vs Actual:** Budget comparisons and profit variances.
3. **Financial Analysis:** Monthly revenue, expenses and net profit trends.

Use the filters to explore the data. Use the navigation buttons to move between sheets.

### 3. Important Notes

- Financial figures are based on synthetic project data.
- Budget figures are illustrative assumptions.
- Dashboard results depend on the selected filters.
- The dashboards are intended for educational and analytical demonstration.
